# PDF Studio — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

PDF Studio is a Chrome extension for reading, editing and converting PDF files.
It is built so that your documents never leave your computer.

## What the extension collects

Nothing. PDF Studio has no server, no account, no analytics, no advertising and
no tracking of any kind. It does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any
personal information, browsing history or document content.

## How your documents are handled

- Every document you open is processed inside your browser, on your own
  machine. Rendering, editing, OCR and conversion to and from Word, Excel,
  PowerPoint and other formats are done by libraries bundled with the extension.
- Files you save are written only where you choose to save them.
- No page, word or image of a document is ever sent anywhere.

## Network access

The extension makes a network request in exactly one case: when you ask it to
open a PDF that is at a web address — by clicking **Edit this PDF** or
**Convert this PDF** while viewing it, or by right-clicking a link to it. It
then downloads that one file, from that address, the same way the browser
itself would, including your existing sign-in to that site so that PDFs behind a
login open. The file is downloaded to your browser and nowhere else.

No other network request is made. All code and data the extension runs is
included in the package; nothing is downloaded or executed from elsewhere.

## Settings stored on your device

The extension remembers a few interface preferences, such as light or dark
theme, in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and are
removed when you uninstall the extension.

## Permissions

- **Access to websites and local files** — used only to download a PDF you
  explicitly ask to open, as described above, and to recognise whether the tab
  you are viewing is a PDF.
- **Context menus** — adds the *Edit* and *Convert in PDF Studio* entries to
  the right-click menu on PDF links and pages.
